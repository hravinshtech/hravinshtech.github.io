Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Hravinsh Cube (the "game"), made by Hravinsh ("we"), handles information. We built the game to work without collecting your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not run servers for this game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information.

Information stored only on your device

Your player name, avatar, optional profile photo, level progress, scores, coins and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

A profile photo is only read when you choose one from your gallery using the system photo picker. The game keeps a copy inside its own storage on your device and never uploads it.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), the game asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Permissions

The game does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. Photo access happens only through the system photo picker when you choose to set a profile photo.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at tech@hravinsh.com and we will help.

Data deletion

Because all game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game. We hold no copy. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the device settings above or Google’s tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address.
