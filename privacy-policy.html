Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Hravinsh Cube (the "game"), made by Hravinsh ("we"), handles information. It applies to players everywhere and of every age.

In short

• We do not collect any personal information. No accounts, no sign-in, no email, no phone number.

• We never ask for your age, email, phone number or any other personal details. The only things you can enter are a player name and a picture — and they stay on your phone.

• We have no servers (no backend). Your player name, avatar, photo, progress and settings are saved only inside the game on your own phone — we never receive them.

• Hravinsh Cube is made for fun. The game is free, and the ads in it are how we, the developer, earn money to keep making it.

• The game is for everyone, including children, so every ad is child-safe for every player: G-rated, never personalized, no tracking.

Information stored only on your device

Your player name, avatar, optional profile photo, level progress, scores, coins and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else by the game.

A profile photo is only read when you choose one from your gallery using the system photo picker. The game keeps a copy inside its own storage on your device and never uploads it.

Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data.

Child-safe ads for everyone

The game does not ask for anyone's age. Because children may play, we treat every player as a possible child:

• Every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent.

• Only ads rated G (suitable for all ages) can be shown.

• Ads are never personalized or interest-based, and the game does not use or request the device advertising ID.

• No ad tracking and no consent pop-ups are needed, because no personal data is used for ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which is certified for use in apps for children and families under Google Play's Families Ads Program.

To deliver the ad and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address, device type and app information. Because every request is child-directed, Google does not use it for personalized advertising or build a profile of the player.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's privacy

Hravinsh Cube welcomes players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — and children can play every part of the game without giving any.

All players — not only children — receive child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated ads, as required for children by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Parents or guardians with any question can contact us at tech@hravinsh.com. Because we store nothing on servers, there is no child data held by us to review or delete; uninstalling the game removes everything on the device.

Privacy laws around the world

We designed the game to respect children's and privacy laws in the countries where it is available, including:

• United States: COPPA and state privacy laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA).

• European Union / EEA: the GDPR, including its special protection for children (Article 8).

• United Kingdom: the UK GDPR and the Age Appropriate Design Code ("Children's Code").

• India: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — no player is tracked or shown targeted ads.

• Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia (Privacy Act) and similar laws elsewhere.

We do this by not collecting personal data at all, not asking anyone's age, keeping game data on your device, and showing only child-safe ads to every player. If the law where you live gives you additional rights, contact us and we will help.

Permissions

The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files. Photo access happens only through the system photo picker when you choose to set a profile photo.

Data deletion

All game data stays on your device, so you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the game. We hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at the same address.
