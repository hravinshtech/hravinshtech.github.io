About JOLVIKO

JOLVIKO: FunZone & More brings brain games, quizzes and daily activities together in one colourful Android app for people aged 7 and older.

What you can do

  • Play 9 puzzle and brain games
  • Take daily and category quizzes
  • Enjoy riddles, facts, quotes, polls and logic challenges
  • Earn points, streaks and achievements
  • Choose Modern or Full Wooden design
  • Use the app in 8 languages
