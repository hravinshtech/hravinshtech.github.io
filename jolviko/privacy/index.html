Privacy Policy

JOLVIKO: FunZone & More by Hravinsh
Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the JOLVIKO: FunZone & More Android app ("JOLVIKO" or "the app"), provided by Hravinsh, handles information. JOLVIKO offers games, quizzes and daily activities for people aged 7 and older.

2. Information we do not collect

JOLVIKO has no account or login. Hravinsh does not ask for or receive your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location through the app. Hravinsh does not operate a server that receives your game data.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device so it can work:

  • Points, streaks, scores, achievements and activity history
  • Quiz, poll and daily activity answers, and favourites
  • Language, theme, sound and reminder choices

Hravinsh cannot access this information.

4. Advertising

JOLVIKO may show ads through Google AdMob. The app treats all users as child-directed for advertising, requests non-personalised, general-audience ads, and removes access to the Android Advertising ID.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction or diagnostic data. Please read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Internet and Google Play services

The app checks whether an internet connection is available before a game starts. This check does not send your game data to Hravinsh. Google Play may process information when you install, update or rate the app, under Google's own policies.

6. Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

7. Sharing

If you choose Share, Android opens its share menu and you decide where the content goes. Hravinsh does not receive what you share.

8. Children's privacy

JOLVIKO is suitable for ages 7 and older. Hravinsh does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us, and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and deleting data

  • Use Profile > Settings > Reset all progress to delete saved progress.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its data from your device.
  • Where shown, use Ad privacy choices in Settings to manage ad consent.

10. Security and retention

App data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond and meet legal obligations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will always appear on this page.
