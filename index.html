Hravinsh Cube

Hravinsh Cube is a block-puzzle game by Hravinsh. Drag pieces onto the board, clear rows and columns, and collect fruits, vegetables and animals across 200 levels — or chase a high score in Classic mode.

The game works completely offline. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
