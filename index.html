Hravinsh Magic Block

Hravinsh Magic Block is a block-puzzle game by Hravinsh, made just for fun and for players of all ages. Drag pieces onto the board, clear rows and columns, and collect fruits, vegetables and animals across 200 levels — or chase a high score in Classic mode.

Classic mode and the first 10 levels work offline; new levels after that need an internet connection. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no servers. The game is free; the ads you see are how we, the developer, earn money to keep making it.
