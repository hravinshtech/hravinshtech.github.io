Hravinsh Biomes Runner — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how Hravinsh Biomes Runner (the "game"), made by Hravinsh ("we"), handles information.

In short

• No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your email, phone number or other personal details.

• We have no servers. Your player name, avatar, progress and settings are saved only on your phone.

• The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob; that is how we earn money to keep making it.

Information stored only on your device

Your player name, chosen avatar, selected runner and world, settings (sound, vibration, graphics, swipe sensitivity) and progress (gems, best score, distance) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded videos (for example +1 life or bonus gems) and occasional ads between runs, delivered by Google AdMob.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and ad interactions. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads, or Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads).

See how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required by law, ads are shown without personalisation.

Permissions

The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files. It uses the internet only to load ads.

Your rights and data deletion

We hold no personal data on servers, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. You can remove all game data at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data. For data processed by Google for ads, use your device ad settings or Google's privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address.
